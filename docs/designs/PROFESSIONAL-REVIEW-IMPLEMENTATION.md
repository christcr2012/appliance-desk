## 1. Consumer lease disclosures, fees and Spanish — W-22, W-25, W-26

**Verified research:** [CFPB definitions](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1013/2/)
screen natural-person household personal-property leases with an initial term
exceeding four months and relevant lessor/obligation criteria; 2026 threshold is
$73,400. Rental-only does not itself exclude Regulation M. The
[disclosure rule](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1013/3/)
requires retainable disclosures before obligation and addresses electronic
consent, segregated presentation and non-English disclosures with English on
request. [Required content](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1013/4/)
includes applicable payment, charges and termination information. See the existing
[ordinary-lease issue map](../business/ORDINARY-APPLIANCE-LEASE-LAW-2026-10-13.md).

**Build:** classify actual contracting party, purpose, initial binding term,
termination obligations, lessor count and dated threshold. Classifier returns
candidate applicable/not applicable/review required with reasons, not legal
certification. A business flag alone cannot rewrite household purpose evidence.
No rent-to-own, purchase credit or title-transfer path is authorized.

Generate one dated, retainable disclosure projection from frozen quote/terms:
equipment identities, rent/payment schedule, initial due and categories, required
other charges, tax/fee assumptions, maintenance, return/default/termination,
total obligations and applicable disclosures. Cite the legal clause/template
revision and classification evidence. Do not paste auto-finance residual-value
fields into ordinary washer/dryer contracts without applicability.

Locale evidence includes content key, underlying policy/terms revision, locale,
review status/reference and digest. A terms change invalidates translation review
only for affected content; never label machine translation professionally approved.
Preserve English availability and the actual signed language. Legal review selects
controlling text and required electronic-consent delivery/retention. Build preview,
download, owner review and stale-signature denial before release approval.

**Proof:** household six/twelve-month, genuine business, month-to-month with and
without minimum-term penalty, threshold/year change, missing party purpose,
retainable disclosures before binding, old/new quote tab, legal/locale revision
mismatch, unavailable translation fallback. The five-day/$5 late fee and negotiated
fees remain configurable owner policy, not a legal safe harbor. No new fee is
assessed on old signed terms. Counsel determines enforceability and actual coverage.

## 2. Renewal, ending and notices — existing B/W notice and agreement work

**Verified research:** the [2021 enacted notice rule](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/2021a_1239_signed.pdf)
includes the 25–40 day window and special continuous-year timing for renewals
shorter than twelve months. Its short bill summary omits details: do not send
monthly reminders merely by interpreting that summary literally.
[SB25-145](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb25-145) adds online cancellation for
online-consented automatic renewal, with limitations on obstructive retention
flows. Recheck the enacted text and effective/applicability provisions for use.

**Build:** retain continuous-contract root, actual initial/renewal dates, agreed
term, material-change version, customer cancellation mechanism and notice rule
revision. Calculate reminder due dates from the actual applicable renewal event,
not a guessed createdAt or every monthly invoice. Keep annual notice, material
change notice, optional retention outreach and payment reminder separate.

A due notice creates one durable intent keyed by agreement lineage + notice
category + event + template revision. Consent/method rules govern delivery.
Provider accepted/failed/unknown and authenticated manual delivery evidence remain
distinct. If legally adequate notice is unverified, do not pretend delivery or
silently impose new terms; expose owner follow-up and the affected renewal gate.
Online cancellation must be directly available without a forced support call or
mandatory retention offer. Rental ending, equipment pickup, final rent and refund
settlement are separate events; clicking cancel does not magically return goods.

**Proof:** 25/40-day boundaries, continuous twelve-month crossing, leap year,
Denver DST, failed/duplicate notices, manual evidence, revoked channel consent,
online cancellation without retention obstruction and future terms only after
the applicable verified notice period. Counsel confirms actual coverage, delivery
methods, calendar computation, termination fee/notice wording and consequences.

## 3. Acquisition, lease duration and out-of-state credit — W-14/W-2/W-15/T

existing USE_TAX_DUE exemption assumption needs verified scope under IN-33, not
another automatic unchecked exemption. Engineering can calculate both branches.

rental assets, swapped unit, renewals and allocation replay. CPA determines actual
election, paid/due eligibility, continuity and jurisdiction credit allocation.
