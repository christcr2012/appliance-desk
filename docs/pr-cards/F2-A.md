# F2-A and F2-B — integrated scenarios as launch evidence (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-F.md (WU-F3 and the 2026-10-09 amendment, IN-69), docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md section 10, docs/PLAN.md Batch F; owner direction 2026-10-17: cover larger bundles and move to engineering completion quickly.
One coherent outcome: the connected business flows are proven by real tests on a throwaway Postgres (and one browser spec), so no scenario is "manually verified" and the launch ledger (F2-D) can cite them.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
### F2-A
- tests/scenarios/01-lead-to-recurring-billing.test.ts: a real lead is created and converted to a customer, an order for that customer is reviewed, signed and paid by a recorded check, the delivery is completed, and recurring billing starts exactly once; it asserts the signed totals, the receipt, the deposit/reserve record, the signing credit applied to the first invoice, the custody (appliance assignment) and the audit rows, using the real domain functions on a throwaway Postgres with Stripe, email and SMS faked at their client boundary.
- tests/scenarios/02-provider-failure-retry.test.ts: a Stripe failure while starting recurring billing is recorded as a provider operation that is never treated as success, a retry after the failure creates exactly one subscription, a retry after success creates none, an uncertain outcome is not blindly repeated, and the stored error text never contains a secret; asserts ProviderOperation states, the call counts on the fake, and the owner-visible To do or issue.
- tests/scenarios/03-maintenance-to-inspection.test.ts: a customer's maintenance request is scheduled, a visit is started and finished, the request status follows the visit, and the appliance moves through the inspection step to available or out of service as the existing rules say; asserts statuses, job and request links, custody and audit rows, and that a customer cannot reach another customer's request.
- tests/scenarios/04-manual-payment-overpayment-refund-statement.test.ts: a manual payment is recorded against an invoice, an overpayment is held as a credit (not lost and not double-counted), a refund is decided and recorded once, and the customer's statement and the owner's invoice view agree to the cent; asserts invoice, receipt, credit and refund totals in integer cents, idempotent replays, and the audit rows.
- tests/scenarios/05-term-end-renew-terminate-autorenew.test.ts: with the Automatic renewals switch turned on only inside the test, a fixed-term agreement reaches its term end and renews, a customer termination and an auto-renew opt-out are honored on the right dates, an opt-out racing the extension leaves the old end date in the fake Stripe (the B2 ordering rule), and reminders, notices and billing-end rows are asserted at each step.
### F2-B
- tests/scenarios/06-staff-lifecycle-inflight.test.ts: a staff member is created, given in-flight work (a scheduled job and an assigned task), then deactivated; their sessions and access stop at once, in-flight work stays visible and reassignable to the owner, nothing is deleted, and role checks (OWNER, ADMIN, STAFF, CUSTOMER) still hold at the query boundary; asserts audit rows and that a deactivated actor cannot write.
- tests/scenarios/07-message-send-failure-reconcile.test.ts: a customer email send fails after the durable business change (for example an estimate approval), the business change is reported as done with the notice pending, a retry does not create a second account or agreement, an uncertain delivery is marked UNCERTAIN or MISSED and reconciled, and a disabled sender is distinct from a thrown one; asserts MessageDelivery and CustomerNotice states and account/agreement counts, with the sender faked.
- tests/scenarios/08-privacy-request-retention.test.ts: a privacy request (export and deletion) is opened for a customer, retention rules keep what the law or open money requires, deletable data is removed or anonymized only where allowed, another customer's data never appears in the export, and the request records who did what; asserts counts, retained-versus-removed tables and audit rows on the throwaway database only.
- tests/scenarios/09-restore-and-media-recovery.test.ts: a backup export taken from the scenario database is restored into a separate disposable database by the real restore path, row counts and key totals match, provider-state tables are restored, credentials are not, and a media inventory sample re-reads with matching hashes; it refuses any non-local target.
- tests/scenarios/11-month-to-month-continuation-and-online-end.test.ts: a fixed term ends into month-to-month, billing continues without a second agreement, the customer ends it online with the required notice, the end date and pickup are created, and billing stops at the right date; asserts agreement status and dates, the subscription end intent, the pickup job and the notices.
- tests/scenarios/12-late-pickup-company-waiver.test.ts: when the company causes a late pickup the owner records the company-delay waiver, rent and late fees for the delay are waived exactly as the waiver says, a pickup that is the customer's fault still charges, and replaying the waiver changes nothing; asserts invoice lines, waiver record and audit rows in integer cents.
- e2e/scenarios/10-owner-workflow.spec.ts: using only the owner's screens (no code or database help), the owner changes a price, publishes site copy, schedules a delivery, records a check payment and decides a deposit refund; the spec is registered in e2e/shards.json and every step shows its saved result and its To do or notice.
- tests/scenarios/13-purchase-to-audit.test.ts: a purchase receipt with four appliances records use tax, the period closes, File now produces a confirmation, and the audit pack lists every serial; asserts the totals in integer cents, the tax proof and that the audit pack matches the purchase.
- tests/scenarios/14-set-lifecycle.test.ts: a package quote becomes an agreement with a washer and a dryer, one returns early, repricing follows the owner's rules, then pickup, inspection, cleaning and available; asserts line prices, the repricing result, custody episodes and the ending chain.
- tests/scenarios/15-ending-to-ready.test.ts: each ending path (term end, early return, termination, cancellation) creates the pickup, inspection and cleaning or repair chain and ends with the appliance available or out of service; asserts the same chain exists for every path and none is skipped.
- tests/scenarios/16-failed-charge-to-recovery.test.ts: an automatic charge fails, a To do appears for the owner, the customer pays now in the portal, the payment applies once and the To do clears; asserts invoice status, To do creation and clearing, and no duplicate charge on replay.
- tests/scenarios/17-portal-journeys.test.ts: a signed-in customer requests a pickup, reports a problem, sees the status of each and pays an open bill, only for their own records; asserts each created record, its status wording source, and that another customer receives no data from any of these paths.
- The connection check (scripts and tests/connection-check.test.ts, extending the route inventory): an automated walk of every record page's Related panel links and every To do item's button asserts each resolves to a real page that exists in the route inventory; a link to a missing page fails the test.

## Rules the scenarios keep
Every file starts with a comment naming its PLAN scenario and ends with explicit assertions on money totals (integer cents), custody and audit rows. Real Postgres only on the disposable test database (the existing CI-and-local-host guard); Stripe, email and SMS are faked at the client boundary; nothing is mocked inside the business logic except where an existing suite already does so for a stated reason. A scenario that cannot pass without changing business behaviour is reported as a finding with its failing test, not bent (BATCH-F stop-and-ask 3). A defect it proves is fixed in the same bundle with its own regression test when the fix is inside approved scope; otherwise it is recorded as an open finding.

## Excluded and deferred
No schema, price, permission or status change; no Stripe LIVE, customer email, SMS, spending or production data. Owner guide and screenshots are F2-C; review discharge, launch ledger and rollback are F2-D; capacity and backup work from F-part-1 are already merged.
