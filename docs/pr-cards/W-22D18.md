# W-22D18 — Signed rental handoff to scheduled delivery

**Scope:** W-22 / Batch W Amendment B selling milestone S6; owner-approved
IN-58 and signing-before-delivery boundary. Depends on D17 (#441), merged
on `main`. This packet closes an owner workflow, not a documentation task.

## Acceptance

A customer's successful **new** signature atomically creates one genuine
unscheduled DELIVERY visit tied to that customer's address and agreement.
Existing owner-created active delivery visits are reused; cancelled jobs
do not count. Early signed renewals do **not** draft a new delivery.
The visit has no assigned time or dispatched worker. The signing payment
obligation and immutable acceptance still complete in the same transaction;
failure rolls back both the signature and delivery draft.

Owner/admin Today prominently shows a dated "Schedule delivery" task linked
to the real job until its schedule is set, completed or cancelled. It
explicitly says signing does **not** prove signing-payment collection.
Staff/customers cannot see the owner finance-context task. The existing
job detail and dispatch schedule are used, not a parallel booking system.

## Evidence

Unit tests cover owner/schedule task queries, bounded total and role refusal.
Existing term/signature real disposable PostgreSQL integration proves that
customer signature creates exactly one real unscheduled, agreement-linked
DELIVERY job; renewals remain separate. Authenticated browser acceptance
exercises an owner's Today link to the job and staff isolation using
disposable, individually cleaned fixtures. Repo CI, accessibility and
production deployment remain separate evidence layers. No customer
notification or scheduling time is generated automatically.

## Remaining W-22

All-in offer/quote and portal disclosures, real Stripe TEST provider
acceptance, and end-to-end signing-payment-to-first-delivery readbacks remain.
CPA IN-79 and counsel decisions, pricing publication, customer notices and
Stripe LIVE are separately gated. No production write, money-out or spending
approval is supplied by this change.
