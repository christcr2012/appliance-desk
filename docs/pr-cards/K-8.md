# K-8 — Estimated cash outlook, customer health and year-end accounting package

Original complete K-8, approved `docs/designs/BATCH-K.md` §3.8 and `docs/MASTER-ROADMAP.md`, frozen against merged K-7 `82862d13e5be402b56807d680487adfa35e88cf0`. Do not confuse estimated dates/totals with actual bank cash. K-CASH follow-ons remain proposed and do not become authorized implementation by this card.

1. OWNER and ADMIN only can read cashForecast, customerHealth and yearEndPackage through current live database roles and authenticated routes. STAFF and CUSTOMER receive no business-level data, and all queries are read-only with bounded records and safe Colorado dates.

2. A 90-day configurable forecast (1–180 days) uses only ACTIVE agreements with successfully started recurring billing and a known real next billing date; monthly line totals reflect documented discounts/amendments and tax estimates use the existing Colorado tax calculation or are explicitly marked unknown pending missing tax context. Forecasted receipts are never described as bank cash or collected funds.

3. Recurring expense templates produce dated future estimated outflows once per template/cycle, with month-end day safety, and match already generated drafts to avoid double counting; speculative expenses are clearly labelled and never posted.

4. Open sales/use-tax filing periods contribute only supported worksheet amounts and known due dates. Missing/undecided worksheet, unconfirmed liability and unknown tax inputs are disclosed as needs-review entries, not invented payables. No filing, payment, provider call or tax estimate presented as confirmed.

5. Customer health distinguishes active, ending and ended agreements, rental lengths based on supported start/end dates and net collected receipts minus refunds/credit movements. Grouping by original lead source only when convertedCustomerId evidence exists, with an explicit unknown bucket; do not infer missing lead attribution.

6. Year-end accepts an actual, bounded YYYY with completed year or current year-to-date and owner Books opening-date guard. Downloadable deterministic safe CSVs include monthly P&L in separate accrual/cash bases, rental fixed-asset register with book depreciation and distinct CPA tax evidence status, sales and use-tax FILED periods by account, source-backed expenses by category, and supplier-paid summary for 1099-marked vendors at the owner-configured threshold.

7. Supplier-paid/1099 candidates are sourced only from confirmed, vendor-linked cash payments, not purchase orders, procurement receipts or unpaid expense drafts. Where purchase/expense supplier-payment matching cannot be proven, show unknown amounts or an explicit incomplete warning; do not fabricate income tax filing eligibility or claim federal threshold verification.

8. All actual/estimated customer, forecast and year-end metrics are registered in METRICS with date basis, calculation, exact sources and drill path. Phone-friendly OWNER/ADMIN screens, downloading links, clear warnings, safe CSV escaping and accessibility preserve existing Books navigation.

9. Pure math and disposable PostgreSQL tests cover role separation, future-cycle boundaries/DST, no billing-before-delivery, forecast-vs-real separation, incomplete worksheet, source-only collection/refund, lead-link gap, book CPA non-inference and 1099 evidence thresholds. Browser/axe and full exact-head CI/review pass; update roadmap/status/owner guide, decisions, database and go-live acceptance. Real-world CPA and deployed provider validation remain separate.

## Source reuse and safeguards

Read-only, source-first: K-7 profitAndLoss/BookAsset; Billing ACTIVE agreements with billingStartedAt + nextBillingDate and contract line amendments; tax filing status/worksheet; Expense/RecurringExpense; paid Receipt/Payment/Refund; Lead.convertedCustomerId; owner-approved vendor1099ThresholdCents and Supplier.is1099Vendor. Every query revalidates active OWNER/ADMIN in the database. No writes, new billing, tax rate fabrication, deposits counted as sales, unpaid obligations marked paid, unapproved marketing email or provider credentials.

Date source is America/Denver; amount source safe integer cents; missing eligibility/source evidence is displayed as review-required. Existing source CSV helper escapes spreadsheet injection. Create secure route(s) that fetch the data under the user's session on every request. Existing K-7 reports and export routes must remain unchanged.

Run preflight and real-DB and all four browser shards via exact-head CI. Address all review and CI findings together, never weaken tests. Never merge before exact-head CI green. This is not authorization for live money, tax filing, SMS/email or production releases.
