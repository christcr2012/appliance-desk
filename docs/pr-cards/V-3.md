# V-3 and V-4 — the bolder look for every public page, Today and the portal home (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-V.md V-5 (other public pages) and V-7 (desk and portal polish); owner direction 2026-10-17 after accepting the home page: "better, but not as big a change as hoped" — go bolder. Prerequisite V-1 and V-2 merged (#490, gate S-V3 accepted with that comment).
One coherent outcome: the whole product reads as one confident evergreen design, with every sentence still editable by the owner and no new data read or behaviour changed.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
### V-3
- A shared page hero (src/components/site/page-hero.tsx) is the opening of pricing, how it works, service area, contact, launch and the city pages: a full-width evergreen band (dark mode: the dark surface) with the cut-band shape, the page's single h1 in the display size, the lede in the lede size, and, where the page has one, its action. It reads only words the page already takes from the website content, so nothing new is hard-coded and every word stays editable; the legal pages keep their fixed text and are not restyled.
- Pricing shows the first published set on an evergreen cut card (the featured price, tabular figures at the price size) with the other appliances as plain cards, the fees and the good-to-know list as hairline ledgers, all from the existing price list and website items; money still comes only from the price list and settings.
- How it works is a large numbered sequence (numerals from CSS counters, so no stray page words), service area lists each city as a large linked row and the ZIP codes as a quiet list, the launch and contact pages keep their forms and behaviour under the new hero, and the city page reuses the same hero with the city name; the contact prefill, launch form, rate limits and all links behave exactly as before.
- tests/public-pages-render.test.tsx and its saved page text are updated and still prove every editable item appears and no stray unowned words exist on every route and variant; tests/public-copy-from-settings.test.ts also scans the new component; a new component test proves the hero's heading level, band shape and dark-mode surface.
- e2e/signature-public.spec.ts (registered in e2e/shards.json) checks that pricing-free public pages (how it works, service area, contact, launch) have no sideways scrolling at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels in light and dark and that the hero action shows a complete keyboard focus ring; the existing generated accessibility scans cover every public route in light and dark.
### V-4
- Today: the headline visits card gets the cut corner (StatCard), and a day timeline (src/components/ui/day-timeline.tsx) places the visits Today already loads by scheduled time on an 8 am to 7 pm axis, each block with its type icon and word, a status in words (Done, Next or the time) and a Now marker; visits without a time or outside the axis stay listed beneath it, and on a phone it shows the existing VisitRow list; it reads no new data and changes no action.
- Needs-your-attention items get a stripe and a word (Urgent, Today or Soon) taken from the existing exception severity (high, medium, low), never by colour alone; money tiles built from METRICS show an Actual or Estimate tag from the metric's kind; tables and numbers keep DataList and tabular figures.
- The desk top bar shows a Search or jump to... box with a visible Cmd K / Ctrl K hint, and the key press focuses the existing search box (no new search, no new data); the customer portal home shows its four existing questions as cards in the same order with Report a problem as an evergreen card with the fresh button, with no change to what any customer can see.
- tests/day-timeline.test.tsx proves placement, the Now marker, Done/Next/time words, untimed and out-of-range visits staying listed, and the phone list; component tests prove the severity words, the Actual/Estimate tags and the search shortcut; e2e/signature-focus.spec.ts (registered in e2e/shards.json) proves the cut shapes and new controls show a complete keyboard focus ring and that Today and the portal home have no sideways scrolling; existing generated accessibility scans cover Today and the portal.
- Before and after screenshots for the public pages, Today and the portal home at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels in light and dark are produced and sent to the owner; docs (DESIGN-SYSTEM, OWNER-GUIDE, STATUS, DECISIONS, rollback notes) describe the merged behaviour; owner acceptance of the bolder look is requested, never claimed.

Today has no money tiles of its own, so the Actual/Estimate tag is added to the money tiles that exist (METRICS-driven business overview). Gate S-V3 is accepted for the home page; the owner is asked again for these screens from the screenshots, and if he rejects the direction work stops rather than iterating blindly. Merge waits for his answer.
