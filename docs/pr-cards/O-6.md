# O-6 — Manage my business

Design: `docs/designs/BATCH-O.md` D-O7. Read-only: no new table, no new write.

## Acceptance
- `/desk/control-center` (owner and admin): about 40 tasks grouped as Website and promotions; Prices and agreements; Customers
  and service; Equipment and purchasing; Money and taxes; Messages to customers; Team and permissions; Health and recovery.
  Each card has a plain label, one sentence saying what opening it lets you do, and a link to the existing screen.
- Search box by intention ("change rent", "fix failed payment", "stop reminders"): plain keyword matching over label,
  summary and synonyms, best match first, at most 12 results. No AI service.
- Admins never receive owner-only cards in the page data, and an admin the owner has limited from "Change business settings"
  does not get the cards that need it. Staff and customers get nothing (the page requires owner or admin).
- "Right now" cards (customer messages, card payments, background jobs, approvals for the owner): states Ready, Needs
  attention, Waiting for you, Can't check right now, Off, with a checked-at time. Things a setting cannot prove (live email,
  live card key) show "Waiting for you", never Ready. One failing source marks only its own card and leaks no error text.
- Linked from Settings (button) and More. Existing addresses are unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/owner-actions.test.ts` (unique ids, every link is a real screen, owner-only cards point only at screens that
  really are owner-only, role and permission filtering, search), `tests/control-center-readiness.test.ts`.

## Not in this card
- Pinned shortcuts and saved views (O-7).
- Readiness for website changes and live prices (needs content revision and pricing readers; add when wanted).
- Any button that does something. Every card only opens the real screen.
