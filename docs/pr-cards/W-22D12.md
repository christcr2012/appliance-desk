## Complete outcome and boundary

The exact agreement's first invoice shows one signing-payment credit, correct
sales tax, the actual amount paid and the remaining balance on both the owner's
invoice and the customer's invoice/print view. An entitlement without verified
application does not produce a credit display. A verified credit does not itself
make an invoice PAID. This is a **read/display and integrated-proof** capability,
not another provider mutation, refund, schema foundation or balance calculation.
Reuse existing invoice/line/payment facts and existing document component. No migration.
Production-line estimates are planning aids, not reasons to split off the DTO or label.
