# W-7 — first-time setup checklist ("Get set up" card on Today) (approved original subsection)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W.md D-W5; docs/PLAN.md "A first-time setup checklist on Today (owner) until complete"; docs/designs/OWNER-DECISIONS-2026-10-13-ENGINEERING.md (W-7: no invented inventory/launch date, private home address or unsupported legally binding terms). Prerequisite W-6 merged (#482).
One coherent outcome: the owner opens Today on a new account and sees one short, ordered list of what is still missing before the business can really run, each item opening the exact screen that fixes it, and the list goes away only when everything is truly in place.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
- A "Get set up" card appears on Today for the owner, and only the owner, until every setup step is done; admins and staff never receive the checklist, and that is enforced where the data is read, not by hiding the card.
- The steps run in this order: business details, service area, prices, tax setup, payments, email.
- Each step shows "Done ✓" or the one thing still missing, in plain words, worked out from what is actually saved (never from a tick box), and opens the exact existing screen where it is fixed.
- Tax setup is done only when the sales tax account number, how often you file, the first filing period, the business address and the use-tax accounts for Colorado and Greeley are all in place, using the same answers as Taxes → Setup.
- Payments and email report what is truly connected (test or live payment key, payment-sync secret, email service, sender address, customer-email switch); the card never turns anything on, sends or charges anything, and never shows a secret.

## Gates and exclusions
No Stripe LIVE, real customer email, SMS, spending or production fixtures. The card only reads what is connected; turning on live payments or customer email stays a separate owner decision (docs/GO-LIVE-CHECKLIST.md). It invents no business details, launch date or inventory figure. W-8 is not part of this bundle.

## Engineering decisions (not owner policy)
- Step state is always computed from saved data; there is no stored "done" flag that could drift from the truth.
- "Done" for business details means the four public details (name, phone, email, address) are filled in and are no longer the starting placeholders such as "[Company Name]".
- Service area is done once at least one city or ZIP code is saved. Prices are done once at least one active appliance or rental set has a monthly price above $0.
- Payments are done when a recognized Stripe key (test or live) and the payment-sync secret are both connected; the line says which mode, and a test key is described as "no real money moves yet". Email is done when the email service key, a real sender address and the customer-email switch are all set.
- "How often you file" always has a starting value (monthly), so it is shown in the Done line rather than asked as a separate question.
- The card hides itself when all six steps are done. Letting an owner skip a step they will never use is recorded in docs/ROADMAP.md, not built here.
