# W-22D10 — first-invoice double-charge barrier (MERGED #422)

**Approved:** Batch W Amendment B §6.2 B/E/H and W-22. W-22D9 adds receipt-backed immutable entitlement. IN-79 CPA remains OPEN; Stripe LIVE disabled; W-23 blocked.

## Verified drift / acceptance

`startRecurringBillingForAgreement` currently creates an automatically billed subscription immediately after delivery. It has no W-22 down-payment check. Stripe may finalize and charge the first rent invoice during subscription creation. A customer who already paid first rent at signing could therefore be charged rent again before the first-invoice credit path exists.

This slice must fail closed before provider preparation and must not misreport an existing subscription as safe. Under the agreement-locked transaction, distinguish historical no-W-22 agreements, explicitly waived/no-down-payment W-22 agreements, unpaid positive signing obligations, and settled receipt-backed entitlements. Any positive down-payment case remains blocked from first Stripe invoice until a separately verified subscription-specific credit application is implemented. Expose the reason through the existing `billingBlockedReason` and delivery handoff outcome; do not invent an invoice, credit, paid state or new financial UI.

## Test/verification

- Legacy, waived and paid-in-full historical flows remain unchanged.
- For signed positive down payment, both awaiting settlement and legitimately settled cases block before provider claims/products/subscriptions, with distinct human follow-up reasons.
- Inconsistent receipt, missing entitlement or altered order fingerprint also blocks; replays and concurrent job triggers have no first invoice provider mutation.
- Unit and disposable PostgreSQL tests required. Exact-head CI, review, no LIVE/paid deployments, docs checklist B.

## Follow-on acceptance

Do not use customer-wide Stripe balance credits or mark credited on subscription create without invoice-line evidence. Partial delivery, refunds/cancellation and delivery hookup proofs remain later W-22 requirements. The card does not claim W-22 completion.
