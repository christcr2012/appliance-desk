# W-14 + W-2 + W-15 — purchase records, one next tax step, tax proof and audit pack (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md D-WB1, D-WB2, §4–§7; docs/designs/BATCH-W.md D-WA6; docs/PLAN.md W-14/W-2/W-15; IN-65, IN-69, IN-71, IN-64 (deferred). Prerequisite W-26 merged (#478). Order in the approved queue: W-26 → W-14 → W-2 → W-15 → W-3.
One coherent outcome: every appliance and part purchase becomes a durable record with its receipt, a single shared answer to "what happens next with the tax", and a per-appliance tax proof that an auditor can export.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
- W-14: one purchase records seller (existing supplier or typed private seller), date, optional receipt number, receipt photo/PDF pages, subtotal, seller tax as printed with its kind, delivery/other charges and notes; each appliance created from it keeps its own model, serial and price and links back to it; the seller page lists the purchases; seller tax split by price sums to the receipt to the cent.
- W-2: `nextPurchaseTaxStep` gives every answer exactly one next step (full tax → done; partial or none → use tax on the right return with its legal due date; tax-free for rent → rent taxed; another state's tax → CPA To do until IN-65; later → To do in 3 days that turns high 7 days before the covering return closes); intake card, appliance page, PO receiving and To do all show the same step; due dates come from the filing calendar, never hard-coded.
- W-15: every appliance shows its tax proof; an audit pack for any date range lists every serial with seller tax or use tax reported (return + confirmation) and its receipt; parts purchases join the records; the retention setting (start 7 years, owner-editable, with ⓘ) never lets a receipt a filed return relies on be deleted.

## Contract notes for this bundle (design choices made here, each recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md)
- Item price is each appliance's acquisition cost; delivery/other charges stay on the purchase and are shown beside the receipt reconciliation but are not added to machine cost or the use-tax base. Whether they are taxable is a CPA answer (listed with IN-65), never guessed in code.
- Only the OWNER saves a tax answer (existing intake rule); an ADMIN may record a purchase with "I'll check the receipt later".
- Receipts are private finance evidence under `purchases/<draft>/`; OWNER and ADMIN only; PDF allowed for purchases only; no AI receipt reading (IN-64 deferred).
- Money is integer cents through `allocateAcrossLines`; dates are Denver business dates; one idempotency key per form.

## Gates and exclusions
No Stripe LIVE, email/SMS, spending, production fixtures or manual release. Real CPA answers (IN-65 credit rules, IN-71 retention, delivery-charge tax) restrict only their named real use; engineering and the To do that asks the CPA ship now.
