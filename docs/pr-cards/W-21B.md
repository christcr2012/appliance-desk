## Build contract

1. Only from an open out-of-service period (the machine already left on a visit) on a line with at least one other
   machine, on an ACTIVE rental. OWNER/ADMIN; locks: customer ledger → agreement → appliance; plan re-checked inside.
2. New line price = min(today's line price, Σ remaining machines' type prices − prepay discount for that many machines).
3. Single prices start the day after the pickup when the customer gave it back for good that day; otherwise on the
   decision day, and the days in between are first closed as out-of-service days (W-21A credit).
4. The machine's assignment ends ("Taken off: customer done with it …", treated as superseded for share maths); a
   `RentalLineAmendment` (old/new price, `effectiveFrom` = next period start, `outOfServicePeriodId`) is written and the
   line's price lowered; with a subscription, a `SUBSCRIPTION_UPDATE` claim `subscription-line-reprice-<amendment>` is
   written in the same transaction and run after commit (reconciliation retries it).
5. Credit for the rest of the already-billed period: (old − new) per day from the single-price start to the end of the
   billing period containing today, late-delivery per-day setting, rounded once, capped; `CustomerCredit`
   `SET_SINGLE_PRICE` via `PUSH_CREDIT` handoff; invoice mirror labels it. Paid in full: repriced, no automatic credit.
6. Screen: the out-of-service page shows the change in dollars before saving ("from Sep 11 the washer is $35 a month
   instead of $60, and the next bill gets a credit of $16.67").
