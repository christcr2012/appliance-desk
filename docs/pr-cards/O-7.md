# O-7 — My workspace (shortcuts, hidden nudges, saved views)

Design: `docs/designs/BATCH-O.md` D-O8.

## Acceptance
- One private row per owner or admin (`OwnerWorkspacePreference`, additive migration `20261024030000_owner_workspace`, deleted with
  the account). Staff and customers cannot use it. Nobody can read or change another person's row (the only input is the signed-in person).
- On **Manage my business** (`/desk/control-center`, section "My workspace"): **Pin to Today** on any card (up to 12), **Unpin**, hide or
  show the three optional Today nudges (goal behind pace, idle appliances, low fleet use), and save or remove views (up to 20,
  name 1 to 60 characters).
- Saved views are built from an allowlist of what the real screens understand: Equipment by status, Visits by status, Tasks by due and
  whose. No URLs, search text, customer names or IDs are stored. A view opens a normal link; it grants nothing.
- Today shows "My shortcuts" at the top (pins and views) and says how many optional notes are hidden, with a Show again link.
  Important items cannot be hidden: the hide list only accepts the three optional notes.
- Permission changes take effect at once: a pin the person can no longer use (owner-only, or an admin limited from "Change business
  settings") is not shown and cannot be added.
- Every save compares the version the person was looking at; a stale or racing save is refused with a plain message and nothing is lost.
- An unreadable stored row never breaks a page (it reads as empty).
- **Start over** shows exactly what will be removed (counts) and then clears the person's pins, views and hidden notes.
- Backup manifest and DATABASE.md updated.
- Tests: `tests/workspace-preferences.test.ts` (strict checks, tolerant read, links, values match the real screens),
  `tests/workspace-integration.test.ts` (real Postgres: versions, racing saves, own row only, staff and customer refused, owner-only and
  limited-admin pins refused, 12-pin limit, optional notes only, saved views, bad filters, garbage row, account deletion).

## Not in this card
- Saved views for Today by category and for jobs by date range (those screens have no such filters yet).
- Reordering pins, sharing views with the team.
