# W-22D4 — atomic signing payment obligation (PR #414)

Approved source: `docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md` §6.2 (fee and first-month down payment collected at signing, never counted twice), W-22; follows merged #413 (`135676d98ed455921c5041df5a2c0845d69db173`). Single-risk-area additive money **obligation evidence**, not collection. CPA IN-79 OPEN; W-23 blocked.

## Drift and exact build contract

- Existing `signAgreement` locks a frozen W-22 order and atomically records `SignatureRecord.signedAt`, agreement state and audit. `getFrozenSigningOrderInTx` independently reconstructs complete multi-location charge evidence. W-22D3 exposes outstanding money but has no durable signed-payment obligation.
- New `AgreementSigningPayment` has `agreementId` unique/FK, versioned order SHA-256, per-agreement setup fee/tax/down-payment/all-in cents, the actual signature timestamp and `status`. It has *no Stripe object, receipt, settled state, customer-facing paid claim or refund*. Only `NOT_DUE` ($0) and `AWAITING_PAYMENT` (positive cents) can exist; DB CHECK enforces valid fingerprint, version 1, nonnegative reconciled integer cents and status/amount match.
- `signedPaymentObligation` derives fields solely from server-verified frozen facts; `signAgreement` inserts it in the *existing locked signing transaction*, immediately after conditional single-winner signature update and before status/audit. Failed insertion rolls back signature/audit/transition; replay never doubles the row. No client-supplied amounts or external calls.
- One migration `20261015150000_w22_signing_payment_obligation` creates this new table. Update `prisma/schema.prisma`, backup model registry and `docs/DATABASE.md` within same PR; schema health discovers all models dynamically.

## Verification and forbidden effects

- Unit `tests/signing-payment-obligation-w22.test.ts`: 45.00 + 1.50 tax + 60.00 down payment = 106.50 exact; zero waiver and negative/mismatch/overflow/fingerprint invalid fail closed.
- Isolated DB `tests/signing-send-guard-w22-integration.test.ts`: real five-machine $35.00 signing creates one `AWAITING_PAYMENT` obligation attributed to that agreement only; zero fee has `NOT_DUE`; duplicate preexisting obligation aborts and restores unsigned state/audit; CHECK rejects invented settled status and tampered cents; no invoice, provider op, receipt, customer communication or Stripe call.
- Required focused unit/DB, migration and restore checks, `check:quick`, repository preflight, exact-head `ci`, reviews/threads and safe merge. No use of Neon or production in fixture tests.

## Successor boundary

W-22D5 may surface the durable `AWAITING_PAYMENT` state in the owner To do with one real agreement link, obligation amount and specific arrange-payment action. A future separately approved W-22 provider/manual settlement slice must add verified cash/receipt evidence and replay-safe per-agreement first-bill credit before introducing `SETTLED` transitions. Neither W-22D4 nor D5 enables payment collection, refund, tax determination or delivery self-scheduling.
