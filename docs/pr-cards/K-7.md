# K-7 — Profit, balance and appliance payback

**Frozen at planning commit before source work.** Original complete K-7 following merged K-6 commit `986dce6d85c3852313b85d231aab2c5fa3d97842`. The source of truth is `docs/MASTER-ROADMAP.md` K-7, `docs/designs/BATCH-K.md` §3.8, §4 and existing approved money/permissions rules. K-8 forecast/customer/year-end and K-CASH are **out of scope**.

## Nine complete acceptance lines
1. OWNER and ADMIN can inspect Books reports under live role checks; STAFF and CUSTOMER cannot access reporting data or directly load report routes. All report functions are read-only, require a verified Books opening date, reject invalid/future/out-of-order Colorado dates, and do not trigger speculative journal postings.
2. Accrual profit-and-loss reports exact integer cents from immutable JournalEntry/JournalLine and LedgerAccount type, including RENTAL and other income, contra-income as reductions, ordinary EXPENSE and depreciation, void/reversal with its actual posting date and source drill-through, without counting balance-sheet, bank transfers, deposits or pass-through sales tax as business profit.
3. Cash-basis profit-and-loss recognizes actual source-linked payments/receipts and cash expenses, separately attributes paid customer invoices to their posted income/discount/tax rows with deterministic cent allocation, excluding unapplied funds, refundable deposits and pure credit moves. Unsupported linkage is explicitly marked unavailable for review, not converted to invented cash profit.
4. Both bases support previous-period and same-period-last-year comparison with valid inclusive Colorado business dates, explained basis labels, signed cents and deterministic account grouping. A closed-month journal posted later uses its actual immutable posting date and exposes any lateForDate source; no silent backdating.
5. Balance snapshot is as-of-date and source-backed: cash/checking/Stripe clearing/undeposited and receivables, deposits and credits payable, card debt, each sales/use tax account, owner contributions and accumulated profits, with rental cost/accumulated depreciation and RETIRED_PENDING_DISPOSITION shown separately. Totals reconcile to the journal debits/credits without double counting retired cost or depreciation.
6. Per-appliance payback uses actual RENTAL and LATE_RETURN invoice line cents apportioned to appliances assigned on each line's business-period start, deterministically splitting odd cents and avoiding duplicate assignment inflation. If assignment links are missing, mark uncovered invoice dollars rather than guessing an appliance allocation.
7. Appliance repair cost uses completed maintenance jobs' sourced parts/labor (respecting itemized-vs-manual parts precedence) plus separately posted appliance-linked nonduplicate expense amounts, and tracks cost, invoiced rent, repair burden, net, book carrying value, trailing-six-month average and payback months/over-repair warnings without treating forecasts or price-list estimates as actual collections.
8. Every displayed report metric is declared in src/domains/reports/definitions.ts METRICS with actual-versus-estimate, date basis, formula, source and drill location. Books exposes usable mobile OWNER/ADMIN profit/loss, balance and payback screens with clear basis, comparisons, unknowns, drill-through, keyboard and axe accessibility, without any payment, tax filing, provider sync, message or production write.
9. Disposable PostgreSQL regressions prove accrual/cash divergence, taxes/deposit exclusion, sign/reversal, closed-period lateness, two-unit and odd-cent split, repaired-vs-unknown cost and balance reconciliation, role isolation; pure unit math and browser 360/1280 light/dark/axe tests pass. Update STATUS, decisions, owner guide, metric definitions, docs and work index; complete exact-head CI and independent review before merge. Deployed live and accountant trial acceptance remain separate pending checks.

## Source and sequencing

Reuse `JournalEntry`, `JournalLine`, `LedgerAccount`, `src/domains/books/post.ts`, existing `METRICS` in `src/domains/reports/definitions.ts`, `BookAsset` from merged K-6, `src/domains/inventory/analytics.ts` for assignment and repair logic, and `businessDateFromKey`/America/Denver. Exact money in integer cents; one post is one source. For actual invoices, use source-linked line items and period-start assignment evidence, not gross monthly list price. Refund/credit/tax must never become fake cash receipts or income. Missing allocation = visible needs-review row, not guessed result. Read-only queries must enforce OWNER/ADMIN in transaction by `assertActiveTeamActor`; no POST to finance, no external sync.

Sequence: freeze docs/packet separately → implement pure finance math → source-backed read models → METRICS registry → accessible Books P&L/balance/payback UI → real-DB/pure/browser tests → update docs, scorecard/route inventories → full exact-head CI and independent review → merge only green → preserve separate deployed/accountant acceptance evidence.
