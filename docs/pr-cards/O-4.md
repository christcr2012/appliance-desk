# O-4 — Price changes with a start date

Design: `docs/designs/BATCH-O.md` D-O4.

## Acceptance
- Settings → **Price changes with a start date** (`/desk/settings/price-changes`, owner and admin): pick an appliance, a new
  monthly price in dollars and a Colorado start day (tomorrow or later, within a year, price at most $10,000), optional
  note. Saved as a waiting change; the price does not change yet. One waiting change per appliance per day.
- Who may: the owner, and an admin unless the owner has taken away "Change business settings" (O-2A). Staff cannot.
  A retired appliance type cannot be scheduled.
- A nightly job (`/api/cron/scheduled-prices`, 10:10 UTC, so after Colorado midnight in summer and winter) applies each
  due change once through the same price save as the Settings screen (`updateAppliancePriceInTx`: row lock, `PricingRule`
  history row, `pricing.update` audit row naming the scheduled change). Later start days win when two are due. A second run
  applies nothing again.
- Signed agreements keep their price (rental lines are never touched). Sent estimates keep the price on their lines; the
  screen says how many estimates are open. Only prices shown and quoted from the start day on are new.
- Cancel is allowed until applied. Once applied or cancelled it cannot be cancelled.
- If a due change cannot be applied (the person who scheduled it was since limited or archived; the appliance type was
  retired), the price is left alone, the change is marked "Could not be applied" with a plain reason on the screen, and the
  nightly run is recorded as failed so it appears in the system issues inbox.
- The Settings price save now also locks the appliance type row and re-checks that the actor is an active owner or admin.
- Backup manifest lists the new table; DATABASE.md, OWNER-GUIDE, ARCHITECTURE cron table and the automation health list
  are updated.
- Tests: `tests/scheduled-prices.test.ts` (pure date and price rules, Colorado day, DST day),
  `tests/scheduled-prices-integration.test.ts` (real Postgres: saves without changing the price, one per day, staff and
  limited admin refused, applies only on the day and once, history and audit rows, later day wins, cancel rules, failure
  paths, rental lines unchanged).

## Not in this card
- Package and set prices (they need the whole package form); change those on the Settings screen. Follow-up if wanted.
- A Today item for a failed change: the failure already shows in the system issues inbox through the automation record.
- Approval limits do not cover price changes (no dollar amount to compare).
