## Drift check

Follow `docs/implementation-contracts/DRIFT-PROTOCOL.md` checklist A. At `9d449f6d`: a swap refuses "old returned, new not
delivered"; a partial pickup on an active rental leaves the line billing unchanged with no credit ("no early return
rule"); there is no local monthly invoice run — credits reach the next bill as Stripe customer-balance credit that the
invoice mirror shows as a `CREDIT` line; `stageSwap` requires the old machine to be at the customer. Design said
`creditedOnInvoiceId`; mechanically the period stores `creditId` (the bill line is the existing credit mirror).

## Build contract

1. **Schema** `OutOfServicePeriod` (agreement, line, appliance, `startedOn`, `startJobId`, `endedOn?`, `endJobId?`,
   `endReason` REPLACED | SAME_MACHINE_BACK | CLOSED_BY_OWNER, `replacementApplianceId?`, `creditId?` unique,
   `closedByUserId?`, timestamps); one open period per machine (partial unique); dates in order; closed ⇔ reason.
   `BusinessSettings.outOfServiceEscalationDays` (1–60, starting value 3).
2. **Opening** (inside job completion, same transaction and locks): a swap that takes the old machine without
   delivering the new one is now allowed — the old machine's custody closes ("Taken for repair"), it goes to inspection,
   the reserved replacement is released, its assignment stays on the line, and a period opens. A pickup of some machines
   while another machine of the rental is still at the customer opens a period for each machine picked up. Taking
   everything back stays the existing early-return path.
3. **Closing**: a swap delivering a same-type replacement for the machine (staging is allowed while the period is open)
   closes it REPLACED on the visit date; the owner can record the same machine delivered back (custody reopens with
   MANUAL evidence, status RENTED) or close it without a replacement — both on a chosen date, never in the future or
   before the pickup. Credit = the machine's share of the line (set price split evenly) × days from the pickup day
   through the day before the close date, per the late-delivery per-day setting, rounded once, capped at what was
   billed; none when billing never started or the rental was paid in full in advance (owner decides). The credit is a
   `CustomerCredit` (`OUT_OF_SERVICE`) pushed to Stripe through a `PUSH_CREDIT` handoff; the invoice mirror labels it.
4. **To do** `OUT_OF_SERVICE` (owner/admin): "Return or replace Pat's dryer (out for repair N days)", high from the
   escalation setting, linking to `/desk/inventory/[id]/out-of-service` (credit-so-far, the three ways forward).
   The appliance page links there too. **Portal** home shows "Your dryer has been out for repair since Oct 3…".
5. **Setting** on Settings → Pickups and deliveries (section 4), explained, recommended 3, restore recommended.
