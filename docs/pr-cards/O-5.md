# O-5 — Goals and alerts

Design: `docs/designs/BATCH-O.md` WU-O5.

## Acceptance
- Settings → **Goals and alerts** (`/desk/settings/goals`, owner and admin). For this Colorado month the owner can set,
  change or remove a target for **money collected** (dollars; counted from receipts by the day received) and **new rentals**
  (count by start date). The page shows the number so far, the percent of target, and "on pace", "behind pace", "goal
  reached" or "too early to tell". One target per measure per month; next month starts empty.
- Pace: a straight line from 0 to the target over the month. "Behind" means under 75% of where the line is today, and never
  in the first 7 days of the month. Reaching the target is always "goal reached".
- Warnings (stored settings, explained on screen, with a **Restore recommended** button): idle appliances (starts at 30
  days, 0 = off) and low fleet utilization (starts off; percent 1–99).
- Today: at most one item per goal that is behind, one item for idle appliances (a count, not one per appliance), and one for
  low utilization. They are "medium" nudges, shown only to people who can see finance. Utilization stays quiet until the
  fleet has at least 14 observable days per appliance.
- Who may change: owner, and an admin unless the owner has taken away "Change business settings" (O-2A). Staff cannot.
  Every change writes an audit row (`goal.set`, `goal.clear`, `settings.alerts`).
- Additive migration `20261024020000_goals_and_alerts`; backup manifest, DATABASE.md, OWNER-GUIDE, route inventory updated.
- Tests: `tests/goals.test.ts` (pace and validation), `tests/goals-integration.test.ts` (real Postgres: set/change/clear with
  audit, bad input, limited admin refused, alert save and restore, idle list, Today items and the quiet first week).

## Not in this card
- Goals for other measures (profit, new customers), goal history charts, and goals for past or future months.
- Emailing or texting a goal alert (Today only).
