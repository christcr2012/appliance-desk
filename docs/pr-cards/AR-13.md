# AR-13 — In-app notifications bell

Design: `docs/designs/APP-REDESIGN.md` 4.3, 4D.7. Build spec: `docs/build-specs/engines/NOTIFICATIONS.md`. Depends on
**AR-1** (top bar) and **AR-2** (To do inbox). Risk: **writes after public actions and Stripe webhooks** — a
notification can never make those fail; **optional internal email** through the existing switches (no live
customer email or SMS is turned on).

## Why
Chris wants to know the moment something happens — a new lead, a quote opened, a rental signed, a payment failed, an
approval waiting — without refreshing Today, and to open the record in one tap. To do stays the list of what needs
doing; the bell is "what just happened".

## Decisions already made (do not re-decide)
- Five kinds only (the design's list). More kinds need Chris (ROADMAP).
- Written after the main action commits, deduplicated per person; missing one is harmless.
- Each person chooses in the app / by email per kind in Your account; email starts off; texts are not offered.
- Read notifications are removed after an owner setting (starting value 90 days); unread are kept.

## Steps (in order)
1. Migration (`Notification`, `NotificationPreference`, `notificationKeepDays`), backup policy, privacy deletion.
2. `src/domains/notifications/{rules,index}.ts` + rules and integration tests.
3. Hook the five events (after commit, `after()` / catch + report) with failure-isolation tests.
4. Kit `NotificationBell` in the top bar; actions; `/desk/account/notifications`.
5. Clean-up in the nightly sweep; Settings › Advanced row for the keep days (SETTINGS.md catalog).
6. `e2e/notifications.spec.ts` in a shard; STATUS; card Progress.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Each event creates one notification per right person; retries and double views create no duplicates (tests).
- [ ] A forced notification failure does not fail the lead form, quote view, signing or webhook (tests).
- [ ] Nobody can read or mark another person's notifications; customers never receive them.
- [ ] Bell count stays current without polling an idle tab; opening an item marks it read and opens the record.
- [ ] Preferences save per kind and channel; email only when chosen and allowed by the existing switches.
- [ ] Axe + 5 sizes, keyboard; `ci` green at the exact head; every review comment read.
