# W-12 — Sales tax return XML upload file (gates IN-62, IN-44)

Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W.md D-WA5. Prerequisite W-10 merged. Gates: IN-62
(Colorado's answer on in-house XML upload) and IN-44 (how non-taxed amounts are
reported on the SUTS return).

One coherent outcome: the sales tax return packet's deduction lines carry the
researched most-likely SUTS reporting as configurable owner settings with
honest provenance, and the "Download the return file" XML upload is fully
designed and gated so it can be built the day Colorado's answer and the XML
schema are in writing.

## Owner decision 2026-10-31 (Chris) — IN-44 researched defaults

Chris directed that IN-44 not sit parked waiting for the CPA: the agent
researches the most likely outcome of the CPA's review and applies it as the
default, fully configurable, honestly labelled. The research
(docs/research/2026-10-31-in-44-deduction-reporting.md, primary sources:
current DR 0100 + Schedule A, DOR Supplemental Instructions, C.R.S.
39-26-713(1)(a), State Auditor 2020-TE14) concludes, per packet bucket:

- EXEMPT_SHORT_TERM_RENTAL → Schedule A line A13-1 "Other exempt sales"
  (identify the exemption on the line), reported as a DEDUCTION. Confidence
  MEDIUM — the Auditor's evaluation supports the line as an either/or with
  A3-1; no DOR publication assigns a lessor's receipts a line. Premise
  corrected by the research: the exemption covers leases of three years or
  less with tax paid on acquisition, not 30 days.
- NOT_TAXED_CATEGORY → A3-1 "Sales of nontaxable services", DEDUCTION.
  Confidence HIGH.
- EXEMPT_CUSTOMER_OTHER → A4-1 "Sales to exempt entities and organizations",
  DEDUCTION. Confidence HIGH for government/charitable/school buyers. (A
  resale buyer is not this bucket — wholesale is A1-1.)
- OUTSIDE_AREA → A2-1 "Sales made to nonresidents or sourced to locations
  outside of Colorado", DEDUCTION. Confidence HIGH for the line, MEDIUM
  overall (sales sourced to a different Colorado location never enter this
  site's gross at all — a packet-construction rule, not a label).

Placement is resolved: line 1 of the DR 0100 includes all sales whether
taxable or not, so every bucket above is included in gross and deducted on
Schedule A — no bucket maps to LEAVE_OUT_OF_GROSS. The defaults are labelled
"Researched default — CPA confirmation still pending" wherever provenance
shows. They are NOT CPA-approved; IN-44 stays open, and when the CPA answers
the owner updates the settings — no code change.

## Frozen acceptance

### Slice S1 — researched defaults, provenance, settings (this build)
- Every deduction bucket the packet can produce (EXEMPT_SHORT_TERM_RENTAL,
  EXEMPT_CUSTOMER_OTHER, OUTSIDE_AREA, NOT_TAXED_CATEGORY) has a researched
  recommended default — Schedule A label plus DEDUCTION treatment — in one
  code table with a plain-words reason and its source, and a packet built for
  an account with no saved labels shows those defaults instead of
  "Not decided — ask your CPA".
- A deduction line whose wording came from the researched default is marked
  "Researched default — CPA confirmation still pending" wherever the packet
  shows it; a line with owner-saved wording is not marked; totals and tax due
  are identical either way.
- The deduction wording is edited per bucket — label text and how the amount
  is reported, in plain words — with the recommended default and its reason
  shown and a per-bucket restore; saving never requires editing JSON.
- New filing accounts start with the researched defaults as their starting
  labels, marked as researched defaults until the owner edits a bucket.
- A bucket with no researched default and no saved label still shows
  "Not decided — ask your CPA" with the packet warning, so an uncomputable
  or unresearched bucket (S-T9) stays visibly undecided.
- **Amendment (Chris, 2026-10-31):** the deduction wording is also editable
  by the business's accountant (the Accountant role) — the CPA the IN-44
  question is addressed to can answer it in the app. The editor lives on its
  own page outside Sales tax setup (the accountant's deny-by-default
  allowlist never opens setup; the two new surfaces are added to that
  allowlist deliberately, in this PR). The dedicated save re-checks the
  actor in the transaction (owner or active accountant; an accountant whose
  access has ended is refused), writes only the deduction labels, and is
  audit-logged with the saver, their role, and the per-bucket old and new
  values. Provenance follows the editor: a bucket an accountant sets or
  confirms is marked accountant-set and its "CPA confirmation still pending"
  label clears; an unchanged bucket keeps its provenance whoever saves.
