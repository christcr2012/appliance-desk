# W-5 + W-6 — money turning points, payment links, and lead/quote follow-ups (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W.md D-W3; docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md 6.7 and 6.8; docs/PLAN.md W-5/W-6. Prerequisite W-4 merged (#481). Owner decision 2026-10-16: Take payment by phone uses Stripe's own invoice page, one bill at a time.
One coherent outcome: every money and sales turning point becomes a dated To do with a working button, and an unpaid bill can be sent as a secure link or paid over the phone on Stripe's page.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
- A failed payment puts one To do "<name>'s payment failed — $X" on the list the same day, and a held payment and a deposit decision each put one To do on the list; every one opens the screen where the decision is made (Retry or record a payment, send a reminder, or the held-payment and deposit buttons).
- Every unpaid invoice that has a Stripe payment page shows "Send payment link" and "Copy link" (hidden once paid, cancelled or written off). The message is filled in from the invoice: what the bill is for, the amount due, the due date, days overdue and any late fee, the account's total balance when other bills are open, and the reason it is sent (reminder, failed payment, balance after a partial payment, or asked for by phone). The owner sees the exact message and can add a short note before sending; the wording is an owner-editable template per reason with a restore button. It sends only through the existing customer-email switch (off: send is refused, Copy link still works), the sent text is kept in the customer's history, only OWNER and ADMIN can use it, and there is no public pay-any-bill page.
- "Take payment by phone" (owner decision 2026-10-16: simple version) opens Stripe's own secure invoice page for that one bill in the owner's browser; the payment is recorded only by Stripe's confirmation through the existing payment recording; the person who took it is audited; a "customer gave permission to save the card" tick records who, when and the script read, with the card default left to Stripe's behavior; OWNER and ADMIN only; no card data enters any Appliance Desk table or log. Spreading one payment over several bills is not part of this version.
- A new lead puts a To do "Contact <name>" on the list, due within the owner's response-time setting (starting value: the same day).
- Marking a lead contacted asks "Next follow-up date?" prefilled two days out, and that date becomes a dated To do.
- A quote the customer approves puts a To do "Turn <name>'s approved quote into a rental" due today.
