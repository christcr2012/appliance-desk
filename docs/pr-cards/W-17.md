# W-17 and W-20 — connected records, cleaning step, and the portal that follows the flows (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md D-WB5 and E3 (W-17), D-WB6 (W-20). Prerequisite W-19 merged (#485).
One coherent outcome: the owner can follow any record to everything connected to it, a returned machine can't be rented before it is cleaned, and the customer sees what is next, what they owe and can pay an overdue bill.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
### W-17
- The owner/admin pages for an appliance, customer, rental agreement, purchase, seller, visit and invoice each show a Related panel listing the connected records (for example an appliance's purchase, seller, rentals, visits and repair requests) as plain-worded links to pages that exist.
- The same seven pages show a History timeline, newest first, built from the activity log, saying what happened, who did it and when in plain words with no raw codes.
- Search finds purchases by seller name or receipt number and sellers by name for owner and admin only (never for staff), and keeps finding appliances by serial number and model.
- A passed inspection moves the appliance to a new Cleaning status instead of straight to Available, Cleaning shows on Today as a To do with a working button, and "Cleaning done" makes it Available.
- A failed inspection drafts exactly one unscheduled repair visit for that appliance and moves it to In repair, and a repeat creates no second visit.
### W-20
- Portal home and Billing show "Your next bill" for each current rental: the rent for the appliances now on it, the date, and a plain note that tax and credits are shown on the bill itself, in English and Spanish.
- An overdue, unpaid invoice that has a Stripe payment page shows a "Pay now" button on the customer's invoice page and in the billing list that opens that invoice's own Stripe-hosted page; only the signed-in customer's own invoices work, nothing is offered for paid or closed invoices, and local invoices keep "Pay as arranged".
- The customer's open delivery, pickup and repair visits each show a short status timeline in plain words (requested, scheduled for a date, done) on the portal home.
- After the customer asks for a pickup, reports a problem or ends a rental, the page says in plain words what happens next, in English and Spanish.
- A package line shows as a set, for example "Washer + Dryer Set (washer #A12, dryer #B07)", on portal home and My rentals.

## Gates and exclusions
No Stripe LIVE, customer email, SMS, spending or production fixtures. Pay now only opens the hosted page Stripe already issued for an existing invoice; no card detail touches the app and no charge is made by the app. Staff pages are unchanged. V-C1 onward is not part of this bundle.
