## Completed customer/owner outcome

On a signed, awaiting-payment agreement, OWNER or ADMIN can review the
immutable exact all-in obligation and explicitly record money ACTUALLY
received by cash, check or bank transfer, with business date and
reference. After submission, the agreement has one audited MANUAL Receipt,
one SUCCEEDED manual attempt, one SETTLED obligation and, if applicable,
one agreement-only immutable first-invoice down-payment entitlement.
The owner sees recorded date and receipt; no generic invoice Payment or
customer-wide credit, provider object or synthetic payment is produced.
STAFF and customers cannot initiate or view the owner financial form.

## Integration and gates

- Use existing prepareSigningPaymentAttempt(agreementId, MANUAL, ownerId),
  finance actor transaction locks, and getReviewedSigningPaymentInTx to
  validate amount/fingerprint/signature under lock. Never accept amount
  from browser; use the same immutable signed amount as checkout.
- Refuse a pending Stripe/UNKNOWN attempt, unsupported method, unsigned
  or stale obligation, altered reference or attempted change after receipt.
  Replay of the exact completed manual attempt returns the original receipt;
  concurrent submissions serialize and must not double-create a receipt,
  attempt, entitlement or audit row. Different attempts or unknown outcomes
  require owner investigation, never silent double collection.
- Use one transaction with receipt MANUAL, attempt update, obligation update,
  conditional SigningDownPaymentCredit and AuditLog. Do not create a
  customer-wide credit, invoice allocation, refund or new billing charge.
- Require explicit checked confirmation that money was received. UI states
  that recording a check is confirmation by the owner, not Stripe settlement.
  Owner-only server action and visible failure feedback; no LIVE or paid API.
- No migration. Preserve IN-79 CPA gate for new charge-bearing agreements;
  do not infer setup-fee tax from this already frozen obligation. IN-75/76
  recommendations do not grant cancellation or money-out permission.
