# W-10 and W-19 — use-tax File now panel and the remaining flow gaps (approved original subsections)
Source: docs/designs/BATCH-W.md D-WA3 (W-10); docs/designs/BATCH-W-AMENDMENT-B.md D-WB4 rows S2, S4, S5, M1, M2, E1 and progress-card buttons (W-19). Prerequisites W-8 and W-9 merged (#484).
One coherent outcome: the owner is told exactly what to type into Colorado's site for the use tax return, and no step from a new lead to a finished rental or a customer request is left without a To do and a button.

## Frozen original complete acceptance
### W-10
- The use-tax return page has a "File now" panel with a button that opens Revenue Online in a new tab and, beside it, the DR 0252 lines in form order, each value with a Copy button, ending with a link to "I filed and paid it"; the app never submits or pays anything.
- "Print the filled-in form" produces the current official DR 0252 PDF with the return's numbers filled in; until the official blank form file is supplied it is recorded as a scoped outside dependency and the page prints the line-by-line numbers instead.
- A runbook, docs/runbooks/colorado-filing-channels.md, records where each Colorado return is filed and paid, the DR 0252 line order and the official source URLs with the date they were checked.
- The panel's numbers come only from the saved filing packet for that period (purchases, tax per area, total), are checked by a test against that packet, and are shown only to owner and admin.
### W-19
- After a lead is converted, the lead page shows "Create quote" and "Start agreement" buttons that open the new quote or agreement for that customer.
- Converting a quote to a rental opens the new draft agreement.
- An agreement waiting for signature for more days than an owner setting (starting at 3, explained on the screen, with a restore button) puts "Follow up: <name> hasn't signed" on Today with a link to that agreement, and clears when it is signed or cancelled.
- A customer's repair request appears on Today at once as "Review <name>'s repair request".
- A customer can ask for a pickup in the portal; it arrives as a pickup request (not a repair) with a draft pickup visit, and the customer is told what happens next.
- A rental that ended any way (term, early return, termination) gets a draft pickup visit and a Today item "Schedule <name>'s pickup" that opens it, plus a button on the agreement page.
- Every step of the agreement progress card has a button that opens the page where that step is done.
