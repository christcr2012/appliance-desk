## Observable business result

An OWNER/ADMIN can open **Review cancellation** on an undelivered rental and see actual agreement-specific signing money received, the ordinary/large-order refund policy, and the exact amount to retain/return. The operator reviews signed terms, selects a permissible decision, supplies an auditable reason, and explicitly approves cancellation. The transaction closes the agreement, returns reservations, records an immutable approved cancellation/refund liability, and surfaces unpaid refund obligations on **Today** and on the agreement page. It never automatically sends customer communication, issues Stripe money-out, or treats a pending refund as returned. An unpaid cancellation records zero refund. After delivery use the existing ending/return workflow.

## Integration and correctness boundaries

- Existing `closeAgreementInTx`, active assignments, staged swaps, and subscription cancellation; no parallel lifecycle.
- Owner/admin role and active team actor verified *inside* the transaction; row-locked agreement and signing obligation, exact fingerprint checked again before submission; one approval per agreement.
- Settlement means an actual immutable signing `Receipt`, not an attempt or signed contract. Held/paid invoices, refundable deposits, already-applied first-invoice credits, previous delivery or begun billing block the shortcut; finance reconciliation is required.
- Ordinary order full refund even after scheduled visit. Large order with actual down payment: default full refund through configurable 72-hour post-signing window, afterward ordinary setup/tax retention and down-payment refund; an explicit documented override can voluntarily refund more. No negative/over-receipt refund.
- `PreDeliveryCancellation` is the financial review/audit record, never provider proof. Pending refunds remain visible on Today. Downstream reconciliation/money-out is separately permissioned.
- New schema/migration is additive and backed up in the existing manifest; settings explain the default and allow owner restoration. No LIVE, provider refund, CPA classification, real customer data changes, emails or SMS.
