## Business outcome

After cancelling an undelivered rental, the owner can separately record
that the **specific approved amount was returned**. The original signing
receipt is verified again and the cancellation must remain cancelled,
undelivered, pending an external refund and unapplied to the first invoice.
The review screen shows approved cents; it does not initiate money-out.
A completed manual check/bank/cash return requires an owner acknowledgement
and a unique evidence reference; the UI labels it owner-attested, **not**
provider-verified. A Stripe TEST return may be marked verified only after
a read-only successful original-charge/currency/exact-amount refund readback
under a TEST key, never in production mode. Stripe LIVE and money-out remain
OFF. No email or SMS is sent.

## Safety and acceptance

- Reuse D16 cancellation and immutable AgreementSigningPayment receipt; do not
  add a second collection or automatic refund ledger.
- Owner/admin, active actor and a locked agreement/cancellation record at commit.
  The browser cannot supply, change, or waive the refund cents.
- Fingerprint detects changed state, another return, amount mismatch or duplicate.
  Reference is globally unique and audit preserves user/time/method/source.
- Today shows only pending returns. Agreement and refund detail show distinct
  `PENDING_EXTERNAL_REFUND`, `RETURNED_MANUAL_RECORDED`, or
  `RETURNED_STRIPE_TEST_VERIFIED`; no false paid evidence.
- Unit: TEST provider match, failure/pending/charge/amount/currency mismatch,
  manual confirmation and reference validation.
- Real disposable PostgreSQL: approved return, refusal without confirmation,
  exact source matching, audit, replay and pending-attention reconciliation.
- Authenticated saved-session browser: owner review and submission, staff and
  customer denial, accessibility, independent database readback, fixture cleanup.
- Merge only exact-head CI, release-policy and completed applicable review gates.
  Production deployment comes only from the checked daily release, not this PR.
