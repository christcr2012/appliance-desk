# K-6 — Rental appliance book assets, depreciation and pending disposition

**Frozen scope:** original complete roadmap K-6, based on verified merged K-5 `005e9fc95c2af5c4b9a73b7eaed4bad26099f3dd`. No invented helper subsection or sales implementation. The approved Batch K D-K9 and resale tax addendum override any older automatic retirement-loss rule.

1. An additive, auditable BookAsset ledger identity records each enrolled owned rental appliance's immutable source acquisition cost, book placed-in-service/start month, useful life, salvage policy and version. Missing or conflicting acquisition evidence refuses enrollment and never fabricates book or tax basis.
2. Pure monthlySchedule computes safe integer-cent, straight-line management depreciation beginning the month after purchase/in-service under the approved ApplianceType lifetime or configured default (60 months) and salvage policy (default 0%); the last month absorbs rounding. Changed life/salvage never changes posted months, only the unposted remainder.
3. OWNER-approved bounded depreciation posting checks live role, book opening date, date cutoff, active appliance and period locks under Serializable transactions; each period creates a unique balanced DEPRECIATION:<YYYY-MM> entry using existing K-2 journal accounts, with replay/concurrent calls idempotent and closed periods handled by existing late-entry rules.
4. An appliance withdrawn from rentable service can be staged as RETIRED with dated SELL / DECIDE_LATER / SCRAP / STRIP / DISPOSE / DONATE_OTHER intent. Retirement stops additional depreciation and preserves exact remaining book carrying value; SELL or DECIDE_LATER records a single balanced transfer to separately mapped RETIRED_PENDING_DISPOSITION, never automatic full-value disposal loss.
5. Already posted legacy retirement/disposal journals, conflicting acquisition sources, a previous transfer or an already completed disposal fail closed with owner-visible review reason; retirement plans/versions are auditable, and the subsequent sale/disposal posting belongs to M-2C, never auto-implemented or recorded speculatively in K-6.
6. OWNER enters dated classification-specific federal/state tax-basis evidence in append-only per-asset history, with UNKNOWN/OWNER_SUPPLIED/CPA_CONFIRMED confidence, signed adjustments, allowed/allowable depreciation, section 179/bonus records and CPA/source reference. The screen strictly separates management book balances from IRS adjusted basis and never invents recapture, tax due, tax rate or filing.
7. OWNER can configure default book life and salvage with meaningful bounds and audit trail and can view/enroll/retire assets and add CPA evidence on a phone-ready Books fixed-assets page. ADMIN has strictly read-only books access, STAFF/CUSTOMER are denied, and no provider credentials, live charges, messages, destructive production writes or tax filing occur.
8. Real disposable PostgreSQL regression covers permissions, concurrent/double period posting, 1-cent rounding and DST Colorado cutoff, retirement carrying-value transfer, no premature write-off, immutable original journal/evidence, audit and CPA uncertainty; focused pure schedule tests, browser/axe and full exact-head CI/review are mandatory.
9. Update STATUS/roadmap/decision log, database and migration notes, owner guide, go-live CPA/retirement acceptance and the stored plan/scorecard honestly. Keep real CPA determination IN-56, trial asset reconciliation, external accountant acceptance and deployed release checks separate from source implementation.

## Verified reuse and implementation order

Source evidence: `Appliance.acquisitionCostCents`, `purchaseDate`, `ApplianceType.depreciationMonths`, `BusinessSettings.depreciationMonthsDefault/depreciationSalvagePercent`, `src/domains/books/posting-rules.ts`'s `depreciationAccrued`, `src/domains/books/post.ts`'s balanced `writeJournal`, `src/lib/team-actor.ts`, account map and closed-month placement. Keep journal source as `APPLIANCE` with `DEPRECIATION:<YYYY-MM>` and one source-linked balanced transfer event. New source account `RETIRED_PENDING_DISPOSITION` records a retained book asset, not loss. Do not take disposition money/capital gains in K-6.

Execution: schema/one forward migration → pure schedule → finance atomic enrollment/depreciation/retirement/tax evidence → phone-first owner/admin screens → real-database and browser tests → docs and exact-head CI/review. All money safe integer cents; use America/Denver business dates. No live data touched.

## External acceptance

Owner/CPA still must supply IN-56 classification/allowed-or-allowable depreciation and trial accounting reconciliation. No self-certification from source tests. Production release follows scheduled policy. Stop/refuse ambiguous tax basis without blocking an otherwise legal future M-2C operational sale.
