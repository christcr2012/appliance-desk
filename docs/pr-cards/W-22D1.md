## Drift and contract

- Existing `checkout.ts#createCheckoutSessionForAgreement` charges legacy deposit/waiver or saves a method using `checkout-agreement-${id}`; it never reads frozen W-22 fee, tax, first-month down payment or order identity. Signature action catches checkout failures; its success is *signature only*, not payment.
- Existing authoritative `getFrozenSigningOrderInTx` verifies complete same-customer siblings, tax evidence and digest; `SigningOfferProof` and immutable artifact bind the accepted version. Checkout-mode legacy fixtures omit W-22 charge rows.
- Proposed, now implemented: `signing-checkout-plan.ts#buildFrozenSigningCheckoutPlan` accepts *already verified* server-only `FrozenSigningEvidence[]`, identifies exactly one agreement and yields setup/tax/down-payment lines and exact cents. It does not itself approve tax treatment, verify a signature or invoke Stripe.
- Changed boundary: legacy `createCheckoutSessionForAgreement` reads W-22 charge/signature indicators and fails **before** tax/provider effects if any exists. No fallback to the old amount. Historical agreements without W-22 evidence behave as before; outstanding new payment path intentionally remains unavailable.
