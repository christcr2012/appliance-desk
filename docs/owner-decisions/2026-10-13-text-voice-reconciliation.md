## Rental pricing, set discounts and return accounting

- **Initial offers:** Refurbished washers and dryers at $35/month per appliance, $60/month for a washer/dryer pair. New/premium/bulk offers remain unpublished; any future offers, margins, eligibility, promotions and fine print require review before publication. Special account prices and discounts must be editable.
- **How to show a set:** Two individually identified, billed and contracted machines with an explicit, visible **set discount**, not an opaque "washer/dryer set" line. Example: washer $35 + dryer $35 − pair discount $10 = $60. The set discount ends for **future eligible unpaid billing** once the set is broken.
- **Return of one set machine:** Credit unused time for the returned machine at its allocated share of the discounted **price already paid**; do not retroactively reclaim a set discount or reprice periods already prepaid. The remaining machine moves to its single-machine terms (including the applicable 6/12-month single-machine discount) on later **unpaid** coverage, not on prepaid coverage. If a full year was prepaid, preserve the prepaid price throughout and credit the unused portion for the returned machine using its share of that prepayment. The actual signed agreement and any applicable law still govern.
- **Refund approval:** The system may calculate amounts/credits automatically, but every actual **cash refund** requires explicit confirmation by the owner or a finance-authorized staff member, with audit history. Calculation is not release of funds. Do not automatically transfer money. Reconcile existing W-21B's single-price changes, any subscription timing and the future W-22 refund path against this updated rule before implementation; do not mislabel existing code as already conforming.
- **Standard administrative setup:** $45 per **new delivery location**, distinct from chargeable delivery, relocation and hookup consumables. For every applicable new order, owner/authorized pricing staff must make an explicit setup-fee decision (full, partial, waiver), with a recorded reason and clear quote/agreement disclosure. New hookup items for new rentals are part of job safety/consumables, not separate sale charges; no extra admin fee merely for ordinary swap/redelivery.
- **Add-on:** No automatic new setup fee when adding a machine to an existing active service address; owner may explicitly set an exception.
- **Relocation:** A distinct $75 **per relocation job** by default, regardless of machine count, owner-configurable and requiring case-by-case owner/authorized approval. Never treat the move as an automatic second $45 new-location admin setup fee. On the job, inspect/reuse serviceable connection items and replace worn/unsafe hoses/washers/vent hose/power cord as needed, recorded as consumables/job work rather than individually billed parts.
- **Large order down payment:** Starting eligibility from prior answer: five or more machines OR a business/property-management customer; first month's rent taken at signing and applied once to that agreement's first bill. Not a refundable security deposit. Keep signing payment accounting, delivery-start billing and immutable invoice credit separate.
- **Deposits/reserves:** No security deposits for typical household renters. A negotiated **refundable security reserve** can separately apply to large/commercial/property-manager accounts, alongside any first-month down payment. Record reserve as a liability, never rent revenue; document and review approved damage deductions, and default to **quarterly replenishment of a shortfall**, configurable per account. Legal and accounting review remain necessary.
- **Regular cancellation before delivery:** Full refund of all collected amounts, **even after scheduling**; each cancellation requires the owner’s review/approval. This supersedes the previously proposed scheduled-delivery setup-fee retention.
- **Large-order cancellation before delivery:** Configurable **72 hours from signing** for a full refund; after that ordinarily keep the applicable setup fee and return the first-month down payment; owner must still decide on each case and can approve full/partial/waived retention as permitted by agreed contract and law. Counsel must review customer-facing legal terms before use.
- **Late fee and other billing:** Flat $5 starting late-payment fee, editable; **no certain grace-period number was established** in the recovered conversation. Partial payments apply to the oldest outstanding obligation first. A customer satisfaction survey, if implemented, should be email-only, no more than quarterly, with rating and optional comments; do not map that to repo IN-59, which means something else.
