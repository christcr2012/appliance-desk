# Offers and pricing policy

Planning edition • October 7 2026 • Proposed values do not change live settings

## Current behavior and proposed catalog

The application currently has $35 single and $60 set base defaults, term discounts and separate delivery, installation and removal fees that default to zero. Those settings and existing signed price snapshots remain authoritative. The following are candidate **final monthly prices before applicable tax**, not additional discounts to apply to those defaults.

| Offer | Single per month | Set per month | Candidate term | Qualification |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Refurbished standard | $35 | $60 | 6 months | Inspected refurbished stock; compatible installation |
| Verified new | $50 | $85 | 6 or 12 months | Documented unused condition at first installation |
| Local flexible | $50 | $85 | 3 months or month-to-month | Core service zone; higher turnover allowance |
| Commercial property-paid | Written quote | $60 / $52 / $45 examples | Negotiated fixed term | Respectively 1–4 / 5–9 / 10+ sets; subject to minimum margin and contract |

Selecting $50 from the consultant's $45–$50 premium range avoids an ambiguous advertised price; it remains a recommendation. Do not infer a single-machine rate by halving a set price. Do not promise matched appearance unless the assigned stock actually matches.

## Candidate fees and security

| Item | Proposed starting rule | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Connection fee | **Superseded 2026-10-10 (IN-74):** setup fee per delivery address, admin $15 + consumables $10 for a single machine or $25 for a set; per set for bulk self-install (W-22) | Explicit installation line; bulk or extra machines quoted separately |
| Core service zone | At most 15 driving miles one way from a private operating origin | Full address, recorded route/manual mileage; not ZIP centroid or tax jurisdiction |
| Extended delivery | More than 15 and at most 30 miles; candidate $50 per visit | Quote delivery and later removal separately; include any promised pickup in total economics |
| Beyond 30 miles | Manual approval and quote | No automatic promise or denial based only on a ZIP |
| Stairs | Candidate $30 per visit for one set or single machine without a usable elevator | Access review still required; quote later removal too; additional flights/equipment assessed individually |
| Refundable security | **Chris 2026-10-10 (IN-73): no deposit on ordinary residential rentals.** Large orders (property managers, apartments; threshold to be set) pay a deposit or down payment | Deposit liability; existing held amount carries on renewal; any top-up separately agreed |
| Setup promotion | Waive or reduce the connection line for eligible standard local offers | Never described as “nothing due” if deposit, prepaid rent or tax remains due |
| Flexible-offer setup | Normally $50, not promotion-eligible | Owner exception permitted with reason and margin preview |

Driving miles are a proposed commercial choice, not a legal rule. Exact boundaries: 15.000 is core; 15.001–30.000 extended; more than 30.000 manual. If location or route evidence is missing, show “Needs delivery review,” not a zero charge. Taxes use the separate verified tax location.

## Quote calculation and disclosure

1. Choose a published offer, term, quantity and qualifying stock; capture the version.
2. Use either that offer's final rates or the existing legacy term-discount calculation, never both. Show the chosen method.
3. Add delivery, installation, stairs and removal commitments, each with the correct charge category. A stairs surcharge must be assigned to its underlying delivery or installation category using the reviewed tax matrix.
4. Apply at most one promotion by default. A customer referral credit and partner commission are separate programs; overlapping benefits require an explicit owner rule.
5. Compute deposit and any prepayment separately from rent and fees. Show total due now, monthly amount, optional charges, minimum term and end-of-term costs.
6. Calculate jurisdiction taxes from charge evidence, then freeze the quote and agreement facts. Unknown tax means review, not assumed exemption.

Promotion caps are claimed atomically at signing, with an expiring hold while the customer reviews. Only an unsigned expired hold returns automatically to availability. A signed promotion is not silently recycled after cancellation; an owner can restore a use with an audit reason.

## Renewals and changes

A rate lock is a specific renewal offer with a duration, eligibility and tax/change disclosures, not a perpetual guarantee. For unchanged equipment/address, propose retaining the agreed rent and waiving repeat connection fees. The existing deposit is transferred, not erased or counted as newly collected. Additional equipment, moves, damaged stock or policy changes need a new reviewed quote.

Keep existing cancellation, early-return and automatic-renewal safeguards. No retroactive price update and no notice-clock reset through a new agreement ID. Existing rentals remain on their signed terms unless changed through the established consent workflow.

## Approval and review

Owner chooses offers to publish under IN-48 and validates zone/access policy under IN-06. Admin may prepare a quote; below-floor overrides require the approval capability defined by Batch O and a recorded reason. Margin floors, fees and promotion limits are settings with explanations and restore behavior. No proposed example is seeded as enabled or applied to historical rentals.

## Configurable templates

These proposed offers and agreement terms are editable template presets, not hard-coded contracts. See [Configurable templates](CONFIGURABLE-TEMPLATES.md). The owner can duplicate and edit supported policies and wording, choose a template per quote and preserve each customer’s accepted version.
