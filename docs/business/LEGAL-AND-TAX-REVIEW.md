Colorado DOR's leases guidance describes the short-term acquisition-tax approach but expressly limits its scope to taxes it administers. Evans' current official page says its city tax applies to leases/rentals. These sources directly contradict the consultant's universal exemption promise. They do not determine every fee or every asset's treatment.

The live system's tax design already includes separate jurisdiction rules. Keep that architecture. The outstanding question whether an unpaid use-tax obligation supports exemption is material: T Amendment D presently models some USE_TAX_DUE units as exempt, while IN-33 still asks the CPA to confirm. This planning work flags that dependency and does not silently alter the approved T design or claim the issue is resolved.

## CPA review packet

Use IN-33 through IN-39, IN-44 and IN-46 rather than creating duplicate tax questions. Provide sample invoices for standard, flexible, premium and commercial rentals, including promotions, stairs/access fees, deposit, early return and an acquisition with unpaid use tax. Ask for a written category-by-jurisdiction decision, effective date and supporting authority.

Specifically confirm treatment of continuous use across renewals, mixed-tax-status equipment sets, equipment swaps, taxes paid before registration, acquisition tax credits, setup bundles, deposit application, partner commissions and any eventual rent-to-own program. Tax return forms, filing cadence and current rules must be verified when filing; the consultant's instruction to remit every local use tax directly to the state is not a safe general procedure.

## Counsel review packet

Prepare approved templates for residential terms, a property authorization, a commercial master and equipment schedule, and a referral partnership agreement. Confirm legal entity and signer authority; ordinary-use service scope; deposit handling; cancellation and early-ending treatment; safe access; damage disputes; premium replacement promises; and public price presentation. Preserve online cancellation throughout any retention offer.

Colorado's SB25-145 summary requires online cancellation for online enrollment and allows retention offers only alongside a direct cancellation route. HB25-1090 adds total-price disclosure requirements. Counsel should confirm their application to the exact household and commercial offers before publication. Do not assume property-manager contracts are outside the renewal rules merely because they are commercial.

The older 45-day marketing suggestion is not used to replace the existing notice window. The source's entry language is not adopted: the published Colorado lease-possession provision conditions nonjudicial recovery on avoiding breach of the peace. For an occupied property or disputed authorization, coordinate with counsel rather than interpreting an app checkbox as permission to enter.

## Proposed language for counsel to refine

**Permission and access.** The customer confirms that installation is permitted at the service address and will help arrange any required property-owner approval. Installation and service occur by arrangement and subject to lawful access. This agreement does not authorize forced entry, lock changes or entry contrary to applicable law. Disputed access will be referred to the business owner for resolution.

**Continuation.** Continued service is governed by the applicable signed renewal or properly consented automatic-renewal terms. Any renewal price or fee waiver is stated in the renewal offer. Existing equipment, deposit balances and service history remain linked. A new document does not erase prior obligations or statutory rights.

**Service scope.** The written quote and agreement specify included maintenance, exclusions and how to request help. Scheduling depends on the stated service policy and safe access. Any additional charge requires a disclosed contractual basis and documented decision.

These are drafting concepts only. Do not paste them over approved production terms or collect signatures on them until their template version has passed legal review.
