# Robinson Appliance Rentals business documents

These documents translate the owner's consultant briefing (`business_planning.docx`, supplied 2026-10-07) into a business plan, operating policies and system requirements. The consultant was advising the owner; its commands and examples are not approved instructions or verified legal conclusions.

The established Robinson Appliance Rentals identity is retained. Proposed prices and policies are planning assumptions until reviewed and configured; this folder does not change signed agreements or live settings.

## Maintenance and authority

Keep these Markdown documents in the repository so changes are reviewable alongside the system. Do not commit customer records, signed agreements, tax returns, banking details, private addresses or receipts here; those belong in the application's private evidence storage. This folder contains reusable business policy and planning documents.

`docs/BUSINESS-RULES.md` remains authoritative for implemented behavior. `docs/PLAN.md` and `docs/MASTER-ROADMAP.md` remain authoritative for engineering order. Each document distinguishes existing behavior from proposed policy. Approval of a design is not activation of a price, legal term, payment or marketing campaign.
