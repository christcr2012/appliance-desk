# Configurable rental options and researched extensions

Owner-requested expansion • October 7 2026 • Planning choices not activated offers

## Recommendation

Give the owner a small set of sensible starting templates with optional policy sections, not one rigid rental model or a page of unexplained switches. Reuse the existing ending, billing, deposit and communication settings. Add options when the underlying financial and operational behavior can support them, and show unavailable options with a clear explanation.

These are options, not twenty-two promises. An optional clause appears only when the associated behavior is supported and selected. New choices that affect money, custody, signatures or external sends need their own tested implementation; a text editor cannot create them.

## Suggested template families

Start with editable draft families: Standard household, Flexible local, Verified-new household, Commercial property-paid and Resident-paid partner referral. Their values are copied from the owner's selected settings or a reviewed example; none is automatically published. A customer-specific template is a duplicate with tracked changes, not an edit to everyone's active contract.

Present the editor in five sections: Equipment and service; Duration and renewal; Charges and payment; Access and delivery; Exceptions and customer protections. Each option shows what the customer will experience, its current/default source, related charges, limits and a preview. Advanced choices remain expandable. Reject contradictory combinations, such as an end-at-term clause with an auto-renew engine selection or a new-replacement promise with no qualifying stock/review route.

## Sources and what they support

Official provider pages retrieved October 7 2026 establish examples of options offered in the market; they do not prove local demand, legal suitability or profitability for Robinson Appliance Rentals.

- [Appliance Warehouse current FAQ](https://www.appliancewhse.com/faq): bundled service, optional limited damage coverage, temporary stays and pickup/transfer assistance. These suggest clear service-scope, coverage and relocation choices.
- [Appliance Warehouse legacy FAQ](https://www2.appliancewhse.com/Faq.aspx): machine/form-factor choices, key-release arrangements and account/service tools. Its fee examples differ from the current bundled-service description; no competitor price or term is adopted as current local pricing.
- [Aaron's current FAQ](https://www.aarons.com/faq): recurring payment arrangements and distinct purchase/return paths. This supports distinguishing payment consent, rental continuation and ownership; it does not authorize a rent-to-own launch here.
- [FTC cost-clarity guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2020/04/when-price-wrong-175-million-ftc-settlement-progressive-leasing-shows-why-cost-clarity-key): identifies deceptive purchase-cost presentation in lease-to-own marketing. This supports showing the full commitment and purchase terms before acceptance rather than only a low periodic payment.

The particular options, starting recommendations, grouping and implementation classifications above are design judgments for this business, informed by those examples and the inspected repository. They are not claims that these providers recommend this system design.
