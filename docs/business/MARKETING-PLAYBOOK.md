# Local marketing playbook

Planning edition • October 7 2026 • Draft copy not approved for publication

## Position and publication rules

Position Robinson Appliance Rentals around convenience, clear pricing and reliable local service. Keep the established Evergreen identity. Use actual inventory photos or clearly labeled representative images. Do not invent reviews, availability, urgency, supplier discounts, comparative claims or a launch date.

Before publishing an offer, verify the exact available rate and term, fees, deposit, applicable-tax wording, service eligibility and inventory. A prelaunch page collects interest and does not promise a delivery date. Use Marketplace only after checking its current eligibility rules for rental listings; channel choice here is a test plan, not a statement that every platform permits this listing. No ads or messages are sent by this document.

Avoid “burst-proof,” “pure profit,” “no hidden fees” alongside undisclosed charges, “guaranteed perfect,” or “free repairs for anything.” Define ordinary-use maintenance coverage and exceptions in the agreement. “Verified new” needs item-level evidence and an agreed replacement policy.

## Draft copy options

### Prelaunch interest

**Laundry rentals are coming to the Greeley area.** Robinson Appliance Rentals is preparing a local washer and dryer rental service. Tell us your address, equipment needs and preferred start date, and we will contact you when we can offer a confirmed quote. Joining the interest list does not reserve equipment or commit you to a rental.

### Standard offer after activation

**A washer and dryer without the purchase upfront.** Ask Robinson Appliance Rentals for a local rental quote with scheduled installation and maintenance coverage under your agreement. We will show you the monthly rent, term, deposit, any service fees and applicable taxes before you decide. Equipment and scheduling are subject to confirmation.

### Approved connection-fee promotion

**Save on your initial connection fee.** Eligible local rentals can receive the connection discount shown in their written quote. Your quote will confirm the offer dates, minimum term, equipment availability, deposit and applicable taxes. The discount does not waive other charges unless the quote specifically says so.

### Verified new offer after activation

**Prefer unused equipment?** Ask about our verified-new washer and dryer offer. We confirm first-use eligibility for the actual machines before promising new equipment. Your quote explains price, term, availability, fees and maintenance replacement terms.

### Flexible offer after activation

**Need laundry equipment for a shorter stay?** Ask about local month-to-month and three-month options. Pricing accounts for delivery and return service. We confirm your address, dates, available equipment and all charges before accepting a booking.

## Campaign workflow and measurement

Create a campaign record before distributing a flyer or ad: name, channel, dates, spend, labor time, destination link/code and associated offer. Keep marketing attribution separate from a discount code and a partner referral code; one identifies where someone came from, another changes a price, another may create a payment obligation.

Record original source, last relevant source before conversion and any self-reported source with provenance. Preserve “unknown.” Never treat typed codes as proof of a partner relationship or overwrite the original source after renewal.

Compare campaigns using paid and fully loaded acquisition cost, signed conversion, first-paid conversion and 90-day contribution by cohort. Organic outreach can have zero ad spend and still consume substantial owner time. Do not add hours to dollars; value hours at an explicitly chosen rate.

## Small experiments

Run one offer change at a time within available stock and service capacity. Candidate experiments are standard quote versus a connection discount, household versus property-manager outreach, and shorter local delivery routes. Define a spending limit and success measure before launch. Stop campaigns when inventory or capacity is exhausted; do not continue collecting paid commitments against an unavailable offer.
