  notices and consent, and early termination fee formula/caps/exceptions. No fee
  amount or renewal collection is authorized by this scope addition.

  actual entitlement/owner approval required; default remains delivery-first.

  release, and the owner override before real inventory can be freed.
- Refund/deduction, unused-term, dispute and liability decisions, including which
  role can approve each financial action and how unknown provider outcomes recover.
- Tax precision, legal/retention/erasure applicability and signing/document evidence.
  Do not encode broad legal claims from the submitted audit as established law.
- Fine-grained staff permissions, offline replay limits, scoring/rescoring policy
  and any paid abuse/map/archive/signature provider selection.
- Actual import data. No meaningful historical import acceptance can use guessed
  customer/inventory records in place of Chris's source file.
