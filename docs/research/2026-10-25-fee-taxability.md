# Research — Are delivery, installation and other fees taxable? (2026-10-25)

Chris, 2026-10-25: "some administrative, delivery, and or installation fees are taxable. I would like to find a way
to have fees that offset my delivery and installation costs, but done in a way that we have the least amount of tax
collection requirements" — for Greeley and "any place within 50 miles of the center of the city of Greeley … and a
way to be sure if I decide to deliver outside of that 50 mile limit."

Confidence: **H** official source read; **M** official source read but not binding or indirect; **U** not verified.
All sources accessed 2026-10-25. Non-binding letters (GIL) are the Department's view, not law.

## Colorado state
- **Rent on short-term leases (≤ 3 years):** no state sales tax on rent when the business paid sales/use tax on the
  appliance when it bought it (C.R.S. 39-26-713(1)(a), https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-26-713; DOR "Leases"
  topic sheet rev. July 2026, https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/documents/SUTT_Leases_Jul_2026.pdf). The
  alternative (buy tax-free with DR 0440 permission, then collect on rent) must be granted before purchase. (**H**)
- **New lease rule:** Special Rule 46 "Leases" adopted 2026-07-07, effective **2026-08-30**, replacing Rules
  39-26-102(23) and 39-26-713-1 (https://tax.colorado.gov/news-article/adopted-sales-tax-on-leases). Its text could not
  be read here (**U** on details); it says economic substance governs.
- **Fees charged with such a lease** (delivery, pickup, installation, haul-away, setup, damage waiver, late fee): the
  topic sheet says all payments under a taxable lease "including any associated charges" are taxable — but when the
  lessor paid tax on purchase the rental is not taxed, and GIL 18-007 says it is then "irrelevant" whether the extra
  charges are part of the rental price (https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/documents/GIL-18-007.pdf).
  **Result: no state tax on rental fees under the pay-at-purchase method.** (**M**)
- **Damage payments** for lost/damaged rental items: not taxable (PLR 17-007, binding only on its requester,
  https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/tax/files/documents/PLR-17-007.pdf). (**M**) Damage *waiver* fees and late fees:
  no specific guidance found (**U**).
- **Used appliance sales (taxable sales):** delivery and installation are left out of the taxed price only when they
  are **separable** (truly optional — the customer may pick up / install themselves) **and separately stated**
  (Sales Tax Guide May 2026, p. 9, https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Sales_Tax_Guide_May_2026.pdf;
  GIL 18-003, https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/tax/files/documents/GIL-18-003.pdf). The charge must be shown **when the
  customer orders**, not only on the later invoice (GIL 15-012 citing Special Regulation 18,
  https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/tax/files/documents/GIL-15-012.pdf). (**H**) A required admin/processing fee on a sale
  is presumably part of the taxed price (**U**).
- **Retail delivery fee:** businesses with $500,000 or less of Colorado retail sales are exempt
  (https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/ST_Retail_Delivery_Fees_Oct_2026.pdf). (**H**) Already handled
  by TFA-2.
- Whether the 3-year treatment also covers **state-collected county and district taxes** (e.g. Larimer County,
  special districts): DOR says lease sourcing also decides state-administered local taxes and the exemption applies
  once "all applicable" taxes were paid at purchase — suggests yes, but the statute names only state tax. (**M/U**)

## Greeley (home rule, collects its own tax)
- Rentals and leases are in Greeley's tax base: the city return covers "ALL SALES, RENTALS, AND LEASES", city rate
  .0411 (city return form, greeleyco.gov content hub). (**M**)
- Ballot measure **2F** (Nov 3, 2026): +0.6% for up to 2067, groceries excluded
  (https://greeleyco.gov/city-clerk/elections/understanding-ballot-measure-2f). (**H**)
- **Not verified (the city code site could not be reached):** whether Greeley has a pay-at-purchase option for
  leases of 3 years or less; whether separately stated optional delivery, installation, pickup and haul-away are left
  out of "purchase price"; whether damage waivers, late fees and admin fees are taxed. (**U**)
- Contact for a written answer: Greeley Sales Tax Division, 970-350-9733, Greeley.SalesTax@greeleygov.com.

## Nearby home-rule cities (found so far; full 50-mile table in `2026-10-25-tax-jurisdictions-50mi.md`)
- **Fort Collins** 4.35% (8.30% total): taxes leases; more than one delivery in 12 months = doing business → license
  (https://www.fortcollins.gov/Business/Sales-Tax/Sales-Tax-FAQs). (**H**)
- **Loveland** 3.0%: taxes leased/rented items; making a delivery or installing = engaged in business
  (https://lovgov.org/services/finance/sales-tax/frequently-asked-questions). (**H**)
- **Evans** 4.5% (8.4% with a state-collected 1.0% fire district): taxes "leases or rentals of items sold in or
  delivered to Evans" (https://www.evanscolorado.gov/your-government/budget-and-finances/sales-and-use-tax/). (**H**)
- **Windsor:** more than one delivery into town in 12 months = engaged in business
  (https://www.windsorco.gov/m/faq?cat=26). (**H**)
- Delivering with your own truck is physical presence, so the economic-nexus thresholds don't protect the business;
  each city's delivery-licensing rule decides.

## What is legal, and what is not
- **Legal ways to keep tax low:** pay tax when buying each appliance (no state tax on rent or rental fees);
  for anything taxable, show delivery, installation, pickup and haul-away as **separate lines with a real "I'll pick
  it up / install it myself" choice**, priced when the customer orders, the same on the agreement and the bill; price
  each service at roughly its real cost.
- **Not allowed / risky:** calling a required fee "optional" when the customer can't really decline it; setting
  delivery or admin fees above cost to move rent out of the taxed amount (substance controls); showing fees only after
  the sale; bundling a sale with installation the customer can't decline.
