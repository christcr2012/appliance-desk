# Late fees — what the law requires (researched 2026-10-25)

Source for FEE-1's legal rules (`docs/build-specs/engines/LATE-FEES.md` "Legal rules"). Accessed 2026-10-25. Not legal
advice; attorney questions are in `2026-10-25-open-decisions.md`. Builds on `docs/business/ORDINARY-APPLIANCE-LEASE-LAW-2026-10-13.md`.
**V** official text read; **U** unverified.

| Rule | Applies to | What it requires | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colorado UCCC delinquency charge (C.R.S. 5-2-203, made applicable to leases by 5-2-102) | **consumer leases**: a person leasing primarily for personal/family/household use, **term over 4 months**, ≤ $75,000, lessor regularly leasing (5-1-301(14)) — i.e. household 6- and 12-month rentals | charge only on an installment **not paid in full within 10 days** after its due date; at most **$15**; **once per installment**; no charge on an installment paid within 10 days even if older ones are unpaid (payments applied to current installments first); **written notice** before the next due date or on the next statement; assessed within 30 days of the due date | V colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_5-2-203, crs_5-2-102, crs_5-1-301 |
| Same — month-to-month household rentals, business/organization renters | term not over 4 months / not a person | outside the UCCC lease rule (inference, **U** — attorney) | — |
| UCCC returned-payment charge (5-2-202) | consumer leases | only if agreed; at most **$25** | V crs_5-2-202 |
| UCCC notification filing (5-6-201/202) | businesses making consumer leases | file with the UCCC administrator within 30 days of starting such leases, then every July 1, with a fee (amount U) | V crs_5-6-201, crs_5-6-202 |
| Rent-to-own law (5-10) | only agreements with an ownership option | not applicable (no ownership option) | V crs_5-10-301 |
| Housing late-fee law (38-12-105: ≥ 7 days late, max greater of $50 or 5%) | landlords of residential premises | likely not Robinson; never write tenant-facing late terms for landlords | V crs_38-12-105, 38-12-502 |
| Leases of goods: agreed damages must be reasonable (4-2.5-504) | all leases | $5 very likely reasonable | V crs_4-2.5-504 |
| Colorado Consumer Protection Act (6-1-105) | all | fee must be disclosed and charged exactly as disclosed | V crs_6-1-105 |
| Automatic renewals (6-1-732, from 2022) | month-to-month rentals renewing each month | clear terms before signing, acknowledgment, easy (one-step online) cancellation, notice of material changes, reminder 25–40 days before renewal past each 12 continuous months | V crs_6-1-732 |
| Regulation M 1013.4(q) | household leases over 4 months (federal) | disclose the late charge amount or method; it **must be reasonable** | V consumerfinance.gov 1013.4 |
| Regulation E 1005.10(d) | autopay from a consumer bank account or debit card | written notice of amount and date **≥ 10 days** before a debit that differs from the previous/authorized amount, unless the customer chose "notify only outside a range" | V ecfr 1005.10 |
| Nacha amount/date change notices (10 / 7 days) | ACH debits | as Reg E; date changes 7 days | U (rulebook paywalled) |
| Stripe ACH mandate | Stripe bank debits | disclose how amounts are calculated or a range; 7 days' notice of timing changes; amounts outside the authorized debits need new authorization | V docs.stripe.com |
| Stripe saved cards | card autopay | terms state timing, how the amount is determined, cancellation; keep the agreement record | V docs.stripe.com |
| Visa recurring reminders | card autopay | reminder ≥ 7 days before a recurring charge when the agreement changed (whether a disclosed fee counts: U) | V Visa flier |
| Colorado surcharge cap (5-2-212, 2%) | card surcharges | a late fee is **not** a surcharge as long as it's the same for every payment method | V crs_5-2-212 |
| TILA/Reg Z | credit sales | not applicable (no ownership, no deferred debt) | V 12 CFR 1026.2 |
| Debt collection (FDCPA, Colo. 5-16) | third-party collectors | collect only in Robinson's own name; fee must be in the signed agreement | V |
| SCRA 50 U.S.C. 3952 | servicemembers who paid before entering service | no termination or taking the appliance back for breach without a court order | V law.cornell.edu |
