# Research — Taxing places within 50 miles of central Greeley (2026-10-25)

Center used: 40.4233 N, 104.7091 W (central Greeley). Distances are straight-line to approximate town centers
(about ±1 mile; "edge" = nearest city boundary when much closer). Rates: DR 1002 rate table, January 2026
(https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/revserials/rev914internet/rev914202601internet.pdf), read through a summarizer
— **Medium** until checked against the live report https://tax.colorado.gov/DR1002; the July 2026 change memo shows no
changes here except Estes Park exemptions and Brighton's service fee (https://tax.colorado.gov/sales-tax-rate-changes).
Accessed 2026-10-25.

**Key finding (High):** in **state-collected** towns and counties, local tax follows the state short-term lease rule —
if the business paid all applicable state and state-administered local tax when it bought the appliance, it collects
**nothing on rent** (https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/documents/SUTT_Leases_Jul_2026.pdf). **Home-rule**
cities set their own rules; those checked tax rent, and many require a city license once the business delivers there
or has rented property inside the city (Colorado Municipal League "engaged in business": more than one delivery in
12 months by a non-common carrier, or owning/renting out property in the city). Only the city's own share is
collected on rent in a home-rule city (Medium; CPA to confirm).

Type: **S** state-collected (no tax on rent with tax paid at purchase; no city license) · **H** home-rule,
self-collected · "not found" = must be confirmed before delivering there (FEETAX-2 readiness).

## Finding the places for an address (for software)
- Official free single-address lookup: https://salestaxlookup.colorado.gov/; a business relying on it with a complete
  address and kept proof is held harmless — for state-collected places and SUTS home-rule cities only
  (https://tax.colorado.gov/gis-hold-harmless-provision) (H).
- API: needs a SUTS account and a business key ("GIS API Key" screen) (https://tax.colorado.gov/GIS-API) (H) —
  existing owner input IN-61. Bulk lookup paused since 2026-09-29, back 2027-01-01 "in a managed capacity"
  (https://tax.colorado.gov/Bulk-Address-Lookup-Pause) (H).
- DOLA special-district boundaries are "not an authoritative source" (https://gis.dola.colorado.gov/CO_SpecialDistrict).
- Approach (FEETAX-2): look up each new address once with the state API, save the result and proof with the date; add
  the home-rule city by city name + county; addresses in non-SUTS home-rule cities (Aurora, Arvada, Boulder, Brighton,
  Broomfield, Denver) are marked for owner review; out-of-state addresses are refused.
- Address → map position for delivery zones: U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, no key in its documented examples
  (https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html) (H for the API shape; terms/limits not stated).
